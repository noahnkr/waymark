# Record format and skills: roadmap

- [standardize-record-lines](../../changes/standardize-record-lines/proposal.md): one record shape `(kind) DATE [args] [by WHO]: text`, written and read by one module; `(blocked)`, `(gap)`, `(note)`, `(accepted)`, `(synced)` and `(override)` as records; a `## Log` section at the end of the task list replaces `## Review` and holds every change-level record; finding places in backticks; `wm check --fix` for the standard layout, including human-written layout but never wording; record format 2, migrated by `wm update`
- tidy-authored-notes: the proposal's human lines as a bullet list, a structured Impact (Specs, User-visible, Code) checked against the deltas, and a structured Outcome; a bullet glossary, numbered goals and constraints, capitalized step headings that may be left out; criteria as a nested list and `- Build: delegate`; `## ADDED Requirements` headings and a renamed section with `From:` and `To:`; `Decided by:` and `Status:` on decisions, with open questions and proposed decisions blocking acceptance; an exploration `Status:` line; a good and a bad example in each template
- merge-milestone-files: `goal.md` and `roadmap.md` become one `milestone.md`; `wm` rewrites Markdown links when it moves a file
- add-step-guidance: `wm instructions` gives a checklist for each drafting step and one preamble of the rules every skill shares; `wm check` enforces what it can
- sharpen-drafting-skills: one layout for every skill; rules for writing requirements and sizing items; a question step and a cross-artifact consistency check in `wm-propose`; fixed summaries for the human; a kickoff interview script; deeper exploration; a severity rubric for the reviewer and stopping rules for the implementer
